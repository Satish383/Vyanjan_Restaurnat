About Us

A Perfect Blend of
Taste, Quality & Tradition

Vyanjan Restaurant is your destination for pure vegetarian dining, freshly baked goods, and traditional Indian sweets. Nestled in Siwan, Bihar, our kitchen brings together the richness of Indian cuisine with the warmth of a family dining experience.

From aromatic paneer dishes and comforting South Indian classics to freshly baked cakes, pastries, and classic Indian mithai — every item is prepared with care, using quality ingredients and maintaining the highest standards of hygiene.
